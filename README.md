# tarot101 🔮
 
A tiny tarot app that draws one of the 22 Major Arcana and tells you what the universe *probably* meant. Results are 100% random and 0% legally binding.
 
**Try it here → [nlhdd.github.io/tarot101](https://nlhdd.github.io/tarot101/)**
 
## How it works
 
1. Click **READ**.
2. Get a card.
3. Interpret it however benefits you most.
4. Click **Retry** if fate was rude.
